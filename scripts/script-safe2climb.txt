Structure the inspection
{CAM:safeHome}A pre-climb inspection needs a clear sequence and a record of what happened. [P0.8] {CAM:safeChecks}{LABEL:Structured checks}Safe2Climb guides the inspector through the pole, attachments, setting, movement and base condition. Each step keeps observations connected to the inspection, making the process easier to review.

Add another safety layer
{CAM:safeUB}The {LABEL:UB1000 check}U B one thousand check adds another layer of inspection evidence. [P0.8] It supplements the physical checks. {CAM:safeScan}The prototype demonstrates acquisition and follow-up states with example results. Those examples are not live measurements, and they do not replace an approved climbing decision procedure.

Record lean and movement
{CAM:safeLean}{LABEL:Lean record}A lean record can retain a photograph and an indicated pole alignment. [P0.8] {CAM:safeMovement}{LABEL:Movement record}Movement observations can include a recorded amount and a note. Together, these records make the observation clearer for another person. This prototype uses an image angle and manually entered movement.

Make issues visible
{CAM:safeIssues}When something causes concern, record the reason and supporting evidence. [P0.8] {CAM:safeRecords}{LABEL:Inspection record}Inspection history keeps issues visible for review and handover, with the pole and inspection context. That creates an auditable record. This local prototype demonstrates the workflow; it does not send a live network notification.
