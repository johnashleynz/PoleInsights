Begin with sound timber
{CAM:whole}An intact surface can conceal deterioration. [P0.8] {CAM:detail}{LABEL:Sound timber}Here the cut face contains sound timber. In bending, fibres farther from the centre have greater leverage. The location of damage therefore matters, as well as its size.

A hollow leaves a shell
{CAM:whole}A cavity removes wood completely. [P0.8] {CAM:detail}{LABEL:Remaining shell}The cut face shows the shell left around the opening. Its size and position affect resistance. Thin shells may split or buckle; those failure mechanisms are not predicted by this beam bending model.
