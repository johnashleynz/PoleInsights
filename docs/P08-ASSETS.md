## Selected end-grain edit prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: provided photograph-style sawn end of a treated pine utility pole. Keep the realistic wood colour, preservative green-grey rim, fine saw marks and detailed natural growth rings. Move the pith/growth-ring centre to about 54% from left and 53% from top: close to central but NOT perfectly concentric. Rebuild natural irregular rings around that pith. Orthographic directly overhead, flat diffuse illumination. The wood face must fill the entire square image edge to edge as an end-grain material texture: continue the growth rings beyond the square boundary, no background, no circular outline, no text, no hole, no labels, no added defects. Crisp photorealistic microscopic wood fibres and light chainsaw scoring. This is an application texture subsequently clipped to the exact pole geometry.

The tool retained more eccentricity than requested; the application's texture mapping recentres and crops the selected texture before clipping it to the true section. A second generated candidate was not selected. Original P03 material remains preserved.

## Defect atlas prompt

Photorealistic square material texture atlas for a timber pole engineering model. Four equal square quadrants with no gutters, no labels and no borders. TOP LEFT: close macro end-grain of a dark sound pine branch knot, resin rich dark amber-brown interlocking very fine concentric rings, slight eccentric central pith. TOP RIGHT: close macro texture of decaying pine wood, fibrous crumbly porous brown wood, mottled pale fungal patches and fine longitudinal cracks. BOTTOM LEFT: dark internal cavity walls in rotten pine, eroded splintered fibres and deeply shadowed porous dark brown recesses, macro photographed. BOTTOM RIGHT: cut face of aged pine with fine grain and natural fibres. Each quadrant must fill its square entirely with wood detail, no object outlines or background, uniform diffuse illumination, no words, no diagram. Real material photographs, not illustrations. These material samples will be masked to exact defect shapes in software.
